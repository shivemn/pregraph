# Entry-level listings on Naukri, November 2026

The receipts behind the "What the job looks like" section on five role pages.

## How they were collected

- One Naukri search per role, filtered to 0 years' experience, India, on 7–8 November 2026.
- The first 40 results were read off the search-results page in a browser: title, company, experience range and the skill tags on each card. No full postings were copied.
- DevOps had few true fresher listings, so it combines the first 20 for "devops engineer" at 0 years with the first 20 for "devops" at 1 year, minus 3 repeats (37 listings).
- Tags were grouped by hand into skills ("MySQL", "PostgreSQL" and "SQL" all count as "SQL and databases") and each listing counted once per skill.
- Naukri cards show at most eight tags, so every count is a floor.

## What this is and isn't

- It's a quick read of what's being asked, not the per-concept tagging in `docs/ROLES.md`. The routes on these pages are still marked draft.
- Search ranking is Naukri's, and sponsored listings come first, so large employers are over-represented.
- Data analyst listings mostly showed 2–7.5 LPA where a salary was given. Salaries aren't shown on the site.
